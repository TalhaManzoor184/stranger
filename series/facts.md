## Confirmed
- Stranger Things season 5 was released in three parts: 4 episodes at Thanksgiving 2025, 3 at Christmas, and the finale on New Year's Eve 2025. (NBC News)
- The finale is Episode 8, "The Rightside Up". Its epilogue jumps about 18 months ahead: graduation, couples together, a last Dungeons & Dragons game. (GamesRadar+)
- Fans named the theory "Conformity Gate" because the ending felt neat and conformist, at odds with the show's outsider themes. (NBC News)
- Who started the theory is unknown. (Know Your Meme)
- A Netflix teaser released at Christmas 2025 carried the date January 7, 2026, but did not mention Stranger Things. (NBC News)
- A die showing 7 is visible near the end of the finale; in season 1 episode 1, Will tells Mike his roll was a seven. (GamesRadar+)
- No secret Episode 9 was released on January 7, 2026. (LBC)
- On January 5, 2026, Matt Duffer said there was no secret extra episode or alternate ending. (NBC News)
- Netflix released the documentary "One Last Adventure" on January 12, 2026. (NBC News)
- A spin-off with new characters is planned, plus the animated "Tales From '85". (NBC News)
- In season 4, Vecna (Henry Creel) pulls victims into visions inside their own minds. (Show plot, season 4)

## Fan claims (always THEORY)
- The finale epilogue is an illusion created by Vecna; a secret Episode 9 would show the real ending.
- Graduates sit with hands folded like Henry Creel (Vecna).
- Orange graduation gowns instead of Hawkins green look like prison uniforms.
- Red EXIT signs behind the "date" scenes hint the world is fake.
- The D&D binders spell "X A LIE" (on screen it reads closer to "X AILE").
- Lucas looking toward the camera saying he doesn't believe in coincidences was a warning to viewers.
- A WHATZIT? board game in the final scene is a clue.
- Searching "Conformity Gate" on Netflix showed Stranger Things, so the episode was hidden there (search matches loosely; not proof).
